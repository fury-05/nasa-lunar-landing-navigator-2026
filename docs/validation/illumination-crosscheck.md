## Why the numbers can differ (none of these has been separated out)
* Window: one year versus an 18.6 year average. The Sun's seasonal cycle repeats yearly, but Earth's libration envelope follows the 18.6 year cycle, so the Earth column is the least comparable.
* Terrain: the reference used a 60 m map from earlier LOLA data, ours uses a 5 m tile and the wide LDEM; a ridge a few metres high matters at a Sun 1 to 2 degrees up.
* Eye height and reference: ours is 1 m above the ground; the reference is a ground-level map. The point may also differ from the reference pixel by up to a pixel.
* Definition: both count a step as lit when any part of the disc is visible, but the reference's disc model and step handling are not reproduced here.
* Terrain beyond the data we read is missing from ours (upper bound), and the reference was computed over its whole 60 m map.
